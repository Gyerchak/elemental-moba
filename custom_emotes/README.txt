Own emotes [U-414]
- One folder per emote: custom_emotes/<name>/emote.png (PNG, at most 256 x 256 pixels and 96 KB)
  and optionally custom_emotes/<name>/sound.wav (at most 256 KB, about 4 s are played).
- The first 4 folders (A-Z) appear in the emote wheel (Ctrl + right mouse drag) after the 6 official emotes.
- They are sent when you lock in your champion. Other players see / hear them only if
  1) the server allows own emotes (host option), and 2) they switched on Options -> Game -> "Show other players' own emotes".
- Official emotes are reskinned with a resource pack instead: resourcepacks/<pack>/emotes/<name>/emote.png + sounds/emote_<name>.wav
